# Assumptions and decisions

Every default the MVP takes where the brief or the wireframes leave a gap, and where to change it.
Items 1–6 are the defaults named in the brief. Items 7–19 are places where the wireframes and the brief
disagreed; the wireframes were followed unless noted. Items 20 onwards are modelling choices made while building.

## Defaults from the brief

| # | Default | Where to change it |
|---|---------|--------------------|
| 1 | The minimum is **S$800 of eligible spend per calendar month**. Pending and Not eligible spend never count towards it. | `minSpendCents` in [`src/config/policy.ts`](src/config/policy.ts) |
| 2 | Only the **flat 0.25%** rate is modelled, also above the minimum, because the tiers are not decided. (The real terms from 1 November 2026, now on the Eligibility screen, pay 6% on dining, groceries, land transport and petrol above S$800. The app's Category 1–8 are placeholders that don't map to those, and no seed month reaches S$800, so every figure shown is what the real terms would pay.) The calculator already takes a list of tiers (picked by the month's eligible total) with optional per-category rates, so tiers can be added without code changes. | `tiers` in [`src/config/policy.ts`](src/config/policy.ts); logic in [`src/domain/cashback.ts`](src/domain/cashback.ts) |
| 3 | **One OCBC 365 card per user**, no card switcher. | `RewardsApi.getCard()` in [`src/api/types.ts`](src/api/types.ts) returns one card; change it to a list to add a switcher |
| 4 | The transaction list defaults to the **current calendar month, up to today** (01 Oct – 12 Oct 2026). | `DEFAULT_PERIOD` in [`src/app/queries.ts`](src/app/queries.ts) |
| 5 | The PDF **downloads as a file** (`cashback-report_<from>_to_<to>.pdf`) and the preview page stays open. | `downloadReportPdf` in [`src/report/pdf.ts`](src/report/pdf.ts) (use `doc.output('bloburl')` to open in a tab instead) |
| 6 | The 6-month history is a **simple bar chart plus a category list**. Tapping a month's bar shows that month's list. | `SixMonths` in [`src/screens/History.tsx`](src/screens/History.tsx) |

## Where the wireframes and the brief disagreed

| # | Decision | Where |
|---|----------|-------|
| 7 | **Category figures are stored, not derived (decision B).** The 13 October transactions cannot produce the wireframe's category numbers: every amount is a whole even dollar figure, so no subset makes S$455 or S$99, and no grouping of the six cashback amounts makes S$0.62 or S$0.45. The Dashboard tiles and History > This month therefore come from a stored category summary, as a bank's categorisation service would send it. It reconciles with the transactions at the total level (S$2,100.00 spend, S$1.55 cashback) and a test proves that. Progress, to-go, cashback and the split bar are always calculated from the transactions. Side effect: filtering Transactions by one category does not add up to that category's figure. | `OCTOBER_CATEGORY_SUMMARY` in [`src/api/seed/october.ts`](src/api/seed/october.ts); gap documented in [`src/api/seed/seed.test.ts`](src/api/seed/seed.test.ts) |
| 8 | **Filter statuses are "Pending" and "Posted"**, as both wireframes show, not the three statuses. Posted = Eligible + Not eligible (both have settled). Rows, labels and the split bar still use exactly Eligible / Pending / Not eligible. | `STATUS_GROUPS` in [`src/domain/filters.ts`](src/domain/filters.ts) |
| 9 | **The no-match example uses Category 7 + 8.** The wireframe's "Posted + Category 1 + Category 3" cannot be empty when Category 1 earned cashback, so `/transactions/empty` opens with Posted + Category 7 + Category 8 + 01 Oct – 12 Oct, which really has no results. | `NO_MATCH_PRESET` in [`src/screens/Transactions.tsx`](src/screens/Transactions.tsx) |
| 10 | **Result count added to the filter sheet.** The wireframe has none; the brief asks for one. "N transactions match" sits in the spare space above Confirm and updates live. | [`src/screens/FilterSheet.tsx`](src/screens/FilterSheet.tsx) |
| 11 | **Retry button added to the unavailable state.** The wireframe has none; the brief asks for one. In the demo, the mock fails once and Retry succeeds. | `UnavailableView` in [`src/screens/Dashboard.tsx`](src/screens/Dashboard.tsx); `consumeOutage` in [`src/api/scenario.ts`](src/api/scenario.ts) |
| 12 | **Bad dates keep the button's look.** The wireframe shows "Get PDF report" unchanged; it is marked `aria-disabled`, does not generate a PDF, and moves focus to the To field. | [`src/screens/Report.tsx`](src/screens/Report.tsx) |
| 13 | **Monthly spend totals for Apr–Aug were read from the bar heights** (160/200/140/220/180/260 px, September = S$2,226.00): Apr S$1,370, May S$1,712, Jun S$1,199, Jul S$1,884, Aug S$1,541. The History chart is spending, not cashback; the cashback figures (Apr 1.12 … Sep 1.21) are used in the report. Category splits for Apr–Aug are invented. | [`src/api/seed/history.ts`](src/api/seed/history.ts) |
| 14 | **"Your usual" values were read from the marker positions:** S$600 / 400 / 350 / 200 / 250 / 150 / 100 / 100. The rule is "above usual = spent so far > usual", which flags only Categories 2 and 5. (The wireframe draws Category 8's bar at S$80 though it is labelled S$99; a usual of S$100 keeps it unflagged, as drawn.) | `SUMMARY` in [`src/api/seed/october.ts`](src/api/seed/october.ts); `isAboveUsual` in [`src/domain/history.ts`](src/domain/history.ts) |
| 15 | **The Eligibility tiles show whole dollars** (S$800, S$160), as designed. Every other amount uses S$1,234.56. | `formatSGD(…, { decimals: 0 })` in [`src/screens/Eligibility.tsx`](src/screens/Eligibility.tsx) |
| 16 | **The wireframe's placeholder rules were replaced with OCBC's real terms**, summarised in plain English (not copied) from the [OCBC 365 cashback terms](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/personal/pdf/cards/tncs-governing-365-cc-cashback-programme-wef1nov26.pdf) (version 1 November 2026) and the [Cards Rewards Transaction Exclusion List](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/personal/pdf/cards/ocbc-cards-rewards-transaction-exclusion-list.pdf) (updated 24 September 2026). There are seven rules, each citing its clauses, and the screen names both documents. These terms start a month after the mock October 2026, when the older two-tier terms (S$80 cap at S$800, S$160 at S$1,600) still applied; the newer ones were used because they match the brief's S$800, S$160 and 0.25%. Each Not eligible transaction links its "Why?" to the exclusion rule that fits it (`ineligibleRuleId`), which opens expanded. The merchants are anonymous, so which exclusion each one hits is our choice. | [`src/config/eligibilityRules.ts`](src/config/eligibilityRules.ts); rule per transaction in [`src/api/seed/october.ts`](src/api/seed/october.ts) |
| 17 | **The bottom tab bar is on every screen**, as in the wireframes (the brief said top-level screens only). Only Rewards works; Home, Plan, Pay & Transfer and More are inert. The PDF preview has no tab bar. | [`src/components/BottomNav.tsx`](src/components/BottomNav.tsx) |
| 18 | **The PDF's "By category" placeholder became a stacked column chart** of cashback per category per month, with a legend that carries each category's total. Colours are fixed per category from a colour-blind-checked palette. | [`src/report/chart.ts`](src/report/chart.ts) |
| 19 | **October transaction categories are our choice**; the brief does not give them. A=2, B=1, C=3, D=1, E=7, F=3, G=2, H=5, I=3, J=2, K=1, L=2, M=6. | [`src/api/seed/october.ts`](src/api/seed/october.ts) |

## Modelling choices

| # | Choice | Where |
|---|--------|-------|
| 20 | Money is held in **whole cents**. Cashback is worked out **per transaction, rounded half up** (S$18.00 → S$0.05, as in the PDF wireframe), summed, then capped at S$160. The rows on screen therefore always add up to the monthly figure. | [`src/domain/money.ts`](src/domain/money.ts), [`src/domain/cashback.ts`](src/domain/cashback.ts) |
| 21 | **Mock clock:** the app always runs as if it is **12 Oct 2026, 22:10 Singapore time**, so the demo matches the wireframes whatever the real date. | [`src/config/clock.ts`](src/config/clock.ts) |
| 22 | The report **defaults to the six full months before the current one** (01 Apr – 30 Sep 2026), as in the wireframe. Dates after today cannot be picked. | `DEFAULT_REPORT_RANGE` in [`src/screens/Report.tsx`](src/screens/Report.tsx) |
| 23 | Reports work for any range: partial months include only the rows in range, months with no transactions are left out, and an empty range says "No transactions in this period." Pending rows show "Pending" in the cashback column. Under decision B, an October report derives its category split from the rows (S$0.61 / 0.48 / 0.46), not from the stored summary. | [`src/domain/report.ts`](src/domain/report.ts) |
| 24 | **Apr–Sep transactions are generated mock data**, built so the real rules reproduce the wireframe figures, including the three April rows shown in the PDF. | [`src/api/seed/history.ts`](src/api/seed/history.ts) |
| 25 | Once eligible spend reaches S$800, the "to go" chip becomes a green **"Minimum met"** chip (not designed). | [`src/screens/Dashboard.tsx`](src/screens/Dashboard.tsx) |
| 26 | Loading shows **skeleton placeholders** shaped like the cards (not designed). The mock waits 350 ms per request so they are visible. | `setMockLatency` in [`src/api/mockApi.ts`](src/api/mockApi.ts) |
| 27 | The PDF uses the built-in **Helvetica** font (Segoe UI cannot be embedded), so it looks slightly wider than the HTML preview. "•" and "·" are drawn as dots because the built-in fonts cannot be relied on for them. | [`src/report/pdf.ts`](src/report/pdf.ts) |
| 28 | The filter sheet always shows a date range. Confirm keeps the dates as a filter (and a chip) only when they differ from the default period or other filters are set. Removing the date chip returns to the default period. | `commitSheetFilters` in [`src/domain/filters.ts`](src/domain/filters.ts) |
| 29 | On wider screens the app is a **centred 480px column** on a warm grey backdrop. | [`src/app/Layout.tsx`](src/app/Layout.tsx) |
| 30 | In the no-card state, screens other than the Dashboard show no data. Only the Dashboard has a designed no-card view. | [`src/api/mockApi.ts`](src/api/mockApi.ts) |
| 31 | The notification bell is decorative (push notifications are out of scope). | [`src/components/AppTopBar.tsx`](src/components/AppTopBar.tsx) |
| 32 | **Motion has two layers.** Screen changes use the View Transitions API (shared-axis drift and fade, crossfades, the sliding sheet); elements inside each screen are choreographed with the Motion library (rise, stagger, count-up numbers, filling bars, springs). Screens play their entrance on first load and when going deeper, not when going back. Reduced motion keeps only short fades; browsers without view transitions change screens instantly. | [`src/motion`](src/motion), [`src/app/navigation.tsx`](src/app/navigation.tsx), end of [`src/index.css`](src/index.css) |
| 33 | **Filters are not history steps.** Opening the sheet adds an entry (so Back closes it). Closing or confirming steps back to the list's entry, and Confirm then updates that entry with the new filters. Removing a chip or Clear all also updates the entry in place. Back from any filtered list therefore returns to the screen before Transactions, not to the unfiltered list. | [`src/screens/FilterSheet.tsx`](src/screens/FilterSheet.tsx), `returnToListWith` in [`src/screens/Transactions.tsx`](src/screens/Transactions.tsx) |
| 34 | **Motion (the animation library) was added to the stack** because CSS alone could not give per-element tweens, springs, exit animations or count-ups cleanly. It is pinned to the 13.x line. Its code is in its own cached chunk. | [`package.json`](package.json), [`vite.config.ts`](vite.config.ts) |
| 35 | **The app opens on a filler banking home at `/`**, modelled on a screenshot the group supplied: greeting, quick actions, product tabs, an account card and promos. **Rewards Tracker replaces Bill payment** and opens the Dashboard; the Cards tab links to the OCBC 365 card there too. The photo is replaced by an original illustration and the PayNow mark by a generic icon (no third-party assets); account and card numbers are the screenshot's obvious placeholders. PayNow, Scan & Pay, Logout, the bell, the gear and the Investments/Loans tabs are inert. | [`src/screens/Home.tsx`](src/screens/Home.tsx), [`src/components/BottomNav.tsx`](src/components/BottomNav.tsx) |
| 36 | **The Dashboard moved from `/` (the brief's route) to `/rewards`** so Home can be the default page, as the group asked. Every other route keeps its brief path. The Home tab goes to `/`, the Rewards tab to `/rewards`; a sub-screen's Back button with no history to return to goes to `/rewards`; unknown paths (including the old `/home`) go to Home. | `routes` in [`src/app/App.tsx`](src/app/App.tsx), [`src/components/BottomNav.tsx`](src/components/BottomNav.tsx), `BackButton` in [`src/components/PageHeader.tsx`](src/components/PageHeader.tsx) |
| 37 | **Rewards Tracker uses the same top bar as Home** (Scan, notification bell, Logout), as the group asked, instead of `Dashboard.png`'s title-and-bell row. The "Rewards Tracker" title now sits under the bar, and the wireframe's red unread dot on the bell is gone because Home's bell has none. The bar is one shared component; it holds still when moving between the two screens. | [`src/components/AppTopBar.tsx`](src/components/AppTopBar.tsx), `DashboardScreen` in [`src/screens/Dashboard.tsx`](src/screens/Dashboard.tsx) |
| 38 | **The filter sheet can be dragged by its handle**, as the group asked (the wireframe only draws the handle). It rests at two heights, its own and full screen; dragging or flicking it below about 30% of its height closes it, like Esc or a tap on the dimmed page. The title row is a second, larger drag area. Tapping the handle, or Enter, Space and the arrow keys on it, switches between the two heights, so dragging is never required. With reduced motion it jumps instead of animating. | [`src/components/BottomSheet.tsx`](src/components/BottomSheet.tsx), thresholds in [`src/components/sheetGesture.ts`](src/components/sheetGesture.ts) |
| 39 | **The expected cashback credit date (US11) defaults to the 16th of the following month**, moved forward to the Monday when that falls on a Saturday or Sunday, because the crediting rule is not decided. OCBC's terms only say cashback is credited in the following month (clause 4(b)). Public holidays are not considered. It shows as "Mon, 16 Nov": no year, with the day in the app's two-digit style ("Mon, 01 Feb"). The mock API returns it with the month's overview (October cashback: Mon, 16 Nov 2026); a real backend would send its own date. It reads "Unavailable" when the rewards data cannot load, and is not shown without a card. Its spacing follows the report's updated Main and Unavailable wireframes, because the PNGs in `design/finished` predate the line. | `CREDIT_DAY_OF_MONTH` and `getExpectedCreditDate` in [`src/domain/creditDate.ts`](src/domain/creditDate.ts); `expectedCreditDate` in [`src/api/mockApi.ts`](src/api/mockApi.ts) |
